Survey count by type, with average rating and low-score surveys.

How these are counted

  • Average rating: the mean of every 1–5 question answered on a survey, then averaged across surveys. Surveys with no ratings are counted but not rated.
  • Low-score survey: any question on the survey rated 1 or 2. Low-score % is of rated surveys.
  • Weeks start on Monday and are labelled by that Monday's date. Periods with no surveys show blank.
  • Older submissions with no survey type but with ratings are counted as Discharge; those with neither are "No type". Surveys with no Freedom facility chosen are "Unassigned".
  • Only summary fields are stored on the server. Respondent names, comments and patient fields in the export stay in your browser.
